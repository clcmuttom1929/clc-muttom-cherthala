WHO WE ARE

A community rooted in Christ and committed to service.

CLC Muttom Cherthala is a Christian Life Community connected with St. Mary's Forane Church, Muttom. We seek to grow in faith, strengthen fellowship and respond to the needs of our parish and wider community.

Through spiritual formation, youth activities, cultural programmes, charitable initiatives and community events, we encourage members to live their faith actively.
