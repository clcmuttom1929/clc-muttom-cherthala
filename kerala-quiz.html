About the event

A tradition of knowledge, curiosity and Kerala

Kerala Quiz commenced in 1993 in connection with Keralappiravi Day celebrations. The 33rd edition is designed to give High School and Higher Secondary School students a platform to test and demonstrate their knowledge, creativity and acumen.

Organised by CLC Muttom

The C.L.C. unit of St. Mary's Forane Church, Muttom, Cherthala is the organiser. CLC (Christian Life Community) is an international association of lay Christians following an Ignatian model of spiritual life and is present in many countries.

Muttom CLC, a unit in the Major Archdiocese of Ernakulam–Angamaly, carries out spiritual and social activities in the community.

What the quiz covers

The questions explore almost everything connected with Kerala, including:

  • History and development
  • Current events
  • Cinema and media
  • Heritage and culture
  • Sports and arts
